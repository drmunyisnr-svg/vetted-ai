## 📥 How to Install the Extension Natively

Because this extension is distributed via our open-source privacy protocol, you can load it directly into your Chromium-based browser (Chrome, Brave, Edge, Opera) using these simple steps:

### 1. Download & Extract
* Click the main **Download Vetted AI Extension (.zip)** button on our deployment page.
* Locate the downloaded `vetted-ai-extension.zip` folder on your computer.
* Right-click the file and choose **Extract All** (or unzip it into a permanent folder on your desktop).

### 2. Enable Developer Mode in Your Browser
* Open a new browser tab and navigate to: `chrome://extensions/`
* Look at the top right corner of the Extensions page and toggle the switch that says **Developer Mode** to **ON**.

### 3. Load the Extension Folder
* Look at the top left corner of the Extensions page and click the **Load Unpacked** button.
* Select the extracted folder on your computer (the folder containing `manifest.json`, `index.html`, and your scripts).
* Click **Open** or **Select Folder**.
